Hold E to raise and Q to lower; release the key to stop the lift. F takes a Pin, a Cup or a whole Cup + Pin stack; next to a Toggle it switches the Toggle. At most one Pin and one Cup: if the Pin slot is occupied, free it first to take another stack. A Cup goes on top of a Pin, not into an empty goal; points appear after the object lands.
